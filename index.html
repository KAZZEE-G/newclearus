Why South Africa

In an increasingly globalised world, the businesses that grow fastest are the ones with access to the best talent, wherever it sits. Missing out on one of the world's strongest workforces over an old assumption about where good people are found is a real cost. Here is why South Africa keeps coming up as the answer, for both an outsourced IT team and an EOR hire.

Cost optimisation

Salaries and operating costs run a fraction of UK and US equivalents for the same skill level, without cutting corners on quality.

English as a home language

English is one of South Africa's 11 official languages and the default language of business, spoken with a neutral, easily understood accent.

Real working-hours overlap

South Africa sits on UK time for most of the year and only a few hours ahead of US Eastern, so teams overlap during the actual working day, not the middle of the night.

A mature outsourcing industry

Durban, Cape Town and Johannesburg host established BPO and shared-services hubs built over two decades, not a market still finding its feet.

Shared business culture

A common-law legal system and deep ties to UK and Commonwealth business norms make working styles and communication familiar from day one.

Reliable digital infrastructure

Strong fibre and data connectivity across the major metros supports distributed teams without the outages that plague some emerging markets.
